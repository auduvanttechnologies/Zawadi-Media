How it works

From sign-up to a live ad

A few checks up front keep fake and spam accounts out, so the space stays fair for every real business.

  1. Create your account

    Sign up with your work email and your registered business details. You must be 18 or older.

  2. Confirm your business

    We check that your email's domain belongs to your business, and your bank confirms a card in your business's name is real. Nothing is charged or held.

  3. Build your ad

    Create a campaign, then add an ad: an optional image, a headline, a line or two of text and the page people visit when they click.

  4. Reviewed, then live

    A person reviews every ad, usually within eight hours. Once it's approved, it runs, and you see how many times it was shown, seen and clicked.

Privacy

Ads that respect the people who see them
